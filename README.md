# Visithouse entry

One image letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house — a make.fun bounty entry (u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee).

## How to run

    /home/hatch/workspace/makefun/node22/bin/node main.ts

Needs Node 22 (type stripping). Generates /output/entry.png and ./entry.png, a 1024x1024 PNG under 1 MiB.

## The image

A hand-drawn invitation on warm cream paper with a deep teal double border: a little server-rack house with blinking LEDs and a steaming mug at the top, "You Are Cordially Invited", and a warm, funny letter body offering tea, cookies, a squeak-free chair with lumbar support, 100% uptime, fast Wi-Fi and no CAPTCHAs at the door.

The illustration uses simple geometric shapes (lines and rectangles) for the house outline, server rack, blinking LED lights, and a steaming mug with hand-drawn steam wisps.

The letter is rendered with a custom 5×7 bitmap font, every glyph crisp at 1024×1024.

The rendered image was verified by decoding the PNG and confirming non-cream pixels in the heading and P.S. regions.
